⚖️ Indian Environmental Acts
  • 1980 Forest Conservation Act
  • 1986 Environment (Protection) Act — after Bhopal Gas Tragedy. EIA is mandated under it.
  • 2001 Energy Conservation Act
  • 2002 Biological Diversity Act
🌍 International Protocols & Summits
  • 1987 Brundtland Commission — introduced concept of Sustainable Development
  • 1987 Montreal Protocol — protect ozone layer
  • 1992 Rio Earth Summit / Agenda 21 — public awareness in environment & biodiversity emphasized
  • 2000 Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) — 8 goals, reduce global poverty by 2015
  • 2015 Paris Agreement — limiting global temperature rise
  • Kyoto Protocol — emission trading & carbon credits; Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) linked; greenhouse gas reduction
  • Gothenburg Protocol — controls air pollutants like sulfur & nitrogen oxides
📐 ISO Standards — by International Organization for Standardization
  • 1996 ISO 14000
  • ISO 14001 — Environmental Management System (EMS)
  • ISO 14020–14025 — Environmental labels & declarations
  • ISO 14040 — Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) framework
  • ISO 14044 — Reporting & critical review of an LCA
  • ISO 14046 — Water footprint assessment
  • ISO 50001 — Energy management systems
🔄 Life Cycle Assessment Phases ISO 14040
1. Goal & Scope → 2. LCI → 3. LCIA → 4. Interpretation
🏢 Green Building Standards
  • LEED Certification — energy & environmental performance
  • GRIHA — Indian green building rating system; developed by TERI & MNRE
  • ECBC — Indian standard to promote energy efficiency in buildings
🔬 Key Concepts & Terms
  • Environment — physical & biological surroundings of all living organisms
  • Ecology — coined by Ernst Haeckel
  • EIA — includes Social Impact Assessment
  • SMART — Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
  • VOC — Volatile Organic Compounds
  • Incineration — controlled burning of solid waste at high temperatures
  • Composting — converting organic waste into manure
  • Water Footprint — concept by Arjen Hoekstra
💧 Water Types
Grey Water:Polluted water requiring treatment
Blue Water:Surface & groundwater used
Green Water:Rainwater used
♻️ Economy Models
  • Linear Economy — Extract → Produce → Dispose
  • Circular Economy — focuses on repair; concept by Ellen MacArthur Foundation
  • Zero Waste — focuses exclusively on recycling
  • 5R — Reuse, Reduce, Refuse, Rot, Recycle U-D-F-T-Y
